Simple, transparent process

How we turn your goals into reality

From first call to launch and beyond — here's exactly what to expect. No surprises, no jargon.

Step 0130-45 minutes

Discovery call

We start with a free consultation to understand your business, goals, and requirements. No sales pressure — just honest conversation about what you need.

Outcome: Clear understanding of your needs and a custom quote.

What happens

Discuss your business and target audience
Understand your goals for the website
Review examples and design preferences
Answer your questions about process, pricing, and timeline
Determine the right package for you
Step 023-5 days

Planning & design

Once you're ready to proceed, we create a detailed plan and design mockups. You'll see what your website will look like before we write a single line of code.

Outcome: Approved design mockups and project plan.

What happens

Create site structure and content outline
Design homepage and key page layouts
Choose colors, fonts, and visual style
Share mockups with you for feedback
Revise based on your input (2 rounds included)
Step 031-2 weeks

Development

This is where the magic happens. We build your website using modern, professional technology — fast, secure, and mobile-friendly.

Outcome: Fully functional website on a staging server.

What happens

Develop all pages based on approved design
Integrate contact forms, WhatsApp, and features
Optimize for mobile devices and speed
Set up SEO foundation (meta tags, structure)
Add your content (text, images, etc.)
Step 042-3 days

Testing & launch

Before going live, we rigorously test everything to ensure your site works perfectly on all devices and browsers.

Outcome: Your website is live and ready to attract customers.

What happens

Test on desktop, tablet, and mobile
Check all forms, links, and features
Run speed and performance tests
Set up Google Analytics and Search Console
Get your final approval
Launch your website live
Step 053-12 months

Support & growth

We don't disappear after launch. You get ongoing support to fix bugs, make updates, and help you grow your online presence.

Outcome: Peace of mind knowing we're here to help you succeed.

What happens

Monitor site performance and uptime
Fix any bugs or issues that arise
Help with minor content updates
Answer questions via email/WhatsApp
Provide guidance on growing your traffic
Option to add features or upgrade anytime

What we need from you

Don't worry — we'll guide you every step of the way.

Your content

Business information, service descriptions, team bios, etc. We'll guide you on exactly what we need.

Brand assets

Logo, colors (if you have them), images, and any design preferences. Don't have these? We'll help.

Timely feedback

Quick responses to our questions and design approvals keep the project moving smoothly.

Don't have everything ready? No problem.

Many of our clients start without finalized content or branding. We'll help you figure it out as we go. The important thing is to get started.

How we stay connected

Regular updates

Progress updates, preview links, and milestone completions. No need to wonder what's happening.

Easy communication

Reach us via email, WhatsApp, or scheduled calls. We typically respond within 24 hours.

Preview links

Access to a staging site where you can see your website as we build it. Review and provide feedback.

Your approval matters

We don't move to the next phase without your approval. It's your website, after all.
